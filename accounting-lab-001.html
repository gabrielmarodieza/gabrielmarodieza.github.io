01Context

A small accounting system, made inspectable.

This guided practice sample follows an invoice register through supplier matching, duplicate checks, payment-review decisions and collections-to-deposit comparison.

It is separate from professional employment results. “Ready” is a sample classification, not authorization to pay.

02Objective
01

Match

Use supplier and invoice keys to identify the record behind each line.

02

Check

Surface duplicates, missing suppliers, approval gaps and timing issues.

03

Reconcile

Compare branch collections with deposits and document unresolved differences.

04

Explain

Leave a review trail that another person can follow in the workbook.

03Formula logic

VLOOKUP

Supplier matching

Connects the invoice register to the supplier reference table so the review starts with the right vendor context.

COUNTIF

Duplicate checks

Checks a supplier-and-invoice key. Identical invoice numbers from different suppliers are not automatically treated as duplicates.

RECONCILIATION

Cash exceptions

Separates timing differences from unresolved short or excess deposits for follow-up.

06Findings & limits

What the exercise makes visible.

  • Review queueApproval, document, duplicate and supplier exceptions remain visible instead of being folded into a total.
  • Cash follow-upShort and excess deposits are separate questions and require separate supporting evidence.
  • Scope boundaryThe workbook is fictional guided practice. It does not establish employer results, payment authority, live AP liability or full-book ownership.
